# Prompt library

Prompts that work well with Stilltone's MCP tools. Replace names and topics with yours.

## Before a meeting
- "Prepare me for my 1:1 with Anna: what did we decide last time, and which of her action items are still open?"
- "Summarize the last three Atlas meetings: decisions, what changed, and open questions. Cite the meetings."
- "What did we promise the customer in calls this month? List each promise with the meeting and the time."

## Decisions
- "List every decision about pricing since September, newest first, with the meeting each came from."
- "When did we decide to move the launch, and what was the reason? Quote the transcript."
- "Which decisions in the Atlas project were made more than once or changed later?"

## Action items
- "Which action items assigned to me are still open? Group them by meeting."
- "Mark the pricing-page action item from the Atlas weekly as done."
- "Who has the most open action items across meetings?"

## Search and writing
- "Find every mention of 'SOC 2' in my meetings and summarize what was said."
- "Draft a follow-up email for yesterday's client call from its decisions and action items."
- "Tag all meetings that mention the onboarding redesign with `onboarding`."

## Keep it tidy
- "In the last meeting, Speaker 2 is Ben. Set the speaker name."
- "Set the project of this week's standups to Atlas launch."
- "Add a note to the Atlas weekly: legal approved the pricing copy."
